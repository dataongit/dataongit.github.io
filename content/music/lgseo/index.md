# Recommendation of the week (probably month)

{{< lead >}}
## Title: Let God Sort Em Out
## Artists: Clipse & Pharrell Williams
## Publisher: Clipse, under exclusive license to Roc Nation Distribution
## Length: 40 Minutes
{{< /lead >}}

![LGSEO](https://is1-ssl.mzstatic.com/image/thumb/Music211/v4/70/a6/9a/70a69aeb-3bcf-968b-de75-f3cb44a89e1b/1605.jpg/1000x1000bb.jpg)


**What an album!** One of rap's greatest duos came with a heavy hitter! Honestly, it might probably be one of the best rap albums of the year, unless someone manages to dethrone it. The production solely done by Pharrell Williams is just phenomenal. 20+ years in the rap/hip-hop genre and he still hasn't lost his touch. Great features by Tyler, Kendrick, Nas, as well as a great hook by John Legend on the first song titled "The Birds Don't Sing". If you are a rap fan, you will be not disappointed.

## Favorite tracks:
- Chains & Whips (feat. Kendrick Lamar)
- P.O.V (feat. Tyler, The Creator)
- So Be It
- Ace Trumpets
- M.T.B.T.T.F
- E.B.I.T.D.A
- Let God Sort Em Out/Chandeliers (feat. Nas)
